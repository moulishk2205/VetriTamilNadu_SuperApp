# IN ORDER (வரிசை): 20-second film treatment

## 1. Title + logline
**IN ORDER (வரிசை)**
Logline: Someone types "I bought land" and it breaks apart into a mess of offices, fees and prerequisites. Then the system puts that mess back in sequence: one plan, one 15-stage workflow, one verified certificate, and one set of data that every level of government can see.

The title word comes from the app itself. Ask Vetri's reply says "வரிசைப்படுத்தியுள்ளேன்" ("I have sequenced").

## 2. Product definition
Vetri Tamil Nadu is a working prototype in which one citizen identity reaches 21 state services. A deterministic planner orders them, and every application runs through one config-driven 15-stage workflow that ends in the citizen's Digital Locker and in role-based dashboards built on the same data.

## 3. Primary problem
One life event, such as buying land, needs several services from several departments, and they must be done in the right order. Each has its own fee, timeline, office and prerequisite. The citizen has to work out that sequence alone.

## 4. Three capabilities, and why these three
1. **Ask Vetri's dependency plan.** It turns a plain sentence into an ordered list of services, which is the INTELLIGENCE beat. It is honest because the planner is deterministic and the claim is only that "it plans the order".
2. **The 15-stage workflow engine (gov.standard.v1).** It carries one real application from citizen to system, officer and signatory, then to the Locker. This is ACTION, shown on a real record: APP-2026-EC-44822.
3. **Five role portals on the same data.** The same activity shows up for Citizen, Officer, Department, MLA and Super Admin. This is IMPACT, and it is the part a Minister owns.

The three map one-to-one onto Plan → Process → Oversight. Each one is the answer to the problem left open by the one before it.

## 5. Central visual metaphor: the thread
- **The idea.** A single 3px gold line, the app's signature gold rule, runs through the whole film.
  - It opens the film.
  - It gets knotted into a tangle of crossing dependency edges (complexity).
  - It is combed straight by the engine.
  - It becomes the vertical spine of the workflow tracker, with one dot per stage.
  - It returns as the 44px gold rule under the final lockup.
- **Why it is true to the product.** Every form the line takes is real UI or real logic:
  - the masthead gold rule;
  - the dependency order Ask Vetri prints;
  - the tracker's vertical timeline (captures 11 and 15);
  - the selector's centred rule.
- **What it means.** Order is literally what the product does: it sequences services and it runs stages in sequence.

## 6. Narrative arc (four acts on a 96 BPM grid, so 1 bar = 2.5 s and the film is exactly 8 bars)
- **Act I, COMPLEXITY (0.0–5.0 s, bars 1–2).** A sentence breaks into the scattered reality behind it: 5 services, 4+ departments, fees, timelines, offices, prerequisites.
- **Act II, INTELLIGENCE (5.0–8.75 s).** The tangle passes through the engine's five stages (intent → context → plan → tools → composer). It comes out as Ask Vetri's dependency-ordered plan.
- **Act III, ACTION (8.75–14.7 s).** We push through the "Encumbrance Certificate →" chip and follow the line:
  - reference APP-2026-EC-44822;
  - the workflow spine;
  - Tmt. R. Kalaivani's "Approve & Issue";
  - the certificate snapping into the Locker.
- **Act IV, IMPACT (14.7–20.0 s).** We pull back to five portals around one record. The record turns out to be Mylapore, and Mylapore turns out to be one point in Tamil Nadu. The film closes on the lockup.

## 8. Transition logic (every change, and what carries the eye)
1. **Black → words:** the caret rides the gold rule. The brand's line literally writes the citizen's sentence.
2. **Words → bubble:** the text's own bounding box becomes the UI bubble. A sentence becomes a request.
3. **Bubble → knot:** every label leaves from inside the bubble. The complexity was hidden inside that one sentence.
4. **Knot → gates:** the knot's own edges are pulled through the gates. The engine works on exactly the mess we just saw.
5. **Combed lines → Ask Vetri card:** the camera rolls 90°, and line heads become list numbers. The order on screen is the plan in the UI.
6. **Card → Acknowledgement:** we push through the chip the citizen clicked, and its crimson carries over. The plan turns into an application.
7. **Status pill → workflow spine:** the pill drops the thread. Status is a position on the line.
8. **Approval dot → officer panel:** the dot opens into the person accountable for that stage.
9. **Generated dot → Locker:** the certificate card itself makes the journey. Output is delivery.
10. **Locker → notification:** the bell in the same masthead reacts. The citizen is told.
11. **Card → data loop:** the record shrinks to the hub of five portals. One record feeds every role.
12. **Hub → map:** the hub is Mylapore, and its spokes become its boundary. Data has a place.
13. **Map → lockup:** the points recede and the thread returns as the title rule. The line that began the film closes it.

## 13. Risks + fallbacks; anti-template measures
1. **Knot combing through the gates (5.0–6.56) is the riskiest shot.** It can read as noisy spaghetti.
   - Mitigation: author only 5 bundles (one per service) plus about 10 drop-out labels, each edge a cubic Bézier whose control points interpolate between two authored poses.
   - Fallback: drop the gates, keep the labels, and have the knot snap straight on the 5.00 hit with the gate labels as a single overline row.
2. **The 400× map zoom shows jagged, simplified polygons at the deep end.**
   - Fallback: start at 40×, with Chennai filling the frame, and let a crossfade-free clip-path iris hand off from the node to the polygon.
3. **About 34 blurred DOM labels in 3D make the render slow.**
   - Fallback: rasterise the knot labels to canvas sprites with pre-baked blur levels.
4. **Push-through-chip scale mismatch at 10.00.**
   - Fallback: a 2-frame full-crimson whip frame motivated by the chip colour, then the stamp.
5. **The counter tick cannot be verified.**
   - Fallback: show a static 3,868 with a single 1px crimson underline that pulses when the spark arrives.
6. **Tamil copy.** Lines 15 and 16 are my own composition and need native review before render.
   - Fallback: use the app's verified line "ஒரே குடிமகன் அடையாளம்" as the Tamil closing line.

**What would make it look like a template, and how this avoids it:**
- **No fades and no centred titles before the end.** Every entrance is a mask reveal or a physical move. Text is left-aligned until the final lockup, which copies the app's own centred selector screen.
- **No particle bursts.** Every flying element is a real, readable label on an authored path.
- **No HUD, no glow on UI chrome, no device frames.** UI is rebuilt flat at real geometry.
- **Brand colours used with discipline.** Gold appears only as rules, small type and the thread. The yellow sparks and the 4% radial rays are the only nods to the client's party palette. They are functional (data moving along spokes) and are gone by 16.3 s, long before the emblem appears.
- **Easing that comes from the product.** The app's splash curve is used only for the two "arrival" moments: the certificate snapping into the Locker and the emblem pop.
- **Pacing that follows real stage sequence.** The camera never cuts. It follows one line from start to finish, so the film reads as one continuous thought rather than a set of slides.
