1. TITLE + LOGLINE

**"One Point, Whole State"**
The camera falls from all of Tamil Nadu onto one point of light in Mylapore. It follows one citizen's question through the government workflow and back up, and the state sees that application counted on the constituency dashboard.

2. PRODUCT DEFINITION

Vetri Tamil Nadu is a working prototype that gives each citizen one identity for 21 state services. Every application runs through one shared 15-stage government workflow, from the citizen to an officer to the citizen's Digital Locker, and officers, departments, MLAs and administrators see it in role portals built on the same data.

3. PRIMARY PROBLEM

A citizen with one life event has to find several services spread across several departments and work out the order to do them in. Buying land means five services across four departments (Registration/IGR, Revenue, local body, water board), and each is a separate counter with separate tracking. Governance only sees the result in aggregate, if it sees it at all.

4. THREE CAPABILITIES (and why)

1. **Ask Vetri plans the order.** "I bought land" becomes five services sorted by dependency. This is the moment the Minister has never seen before. It is a deterministic plan, not generative AI.
2. **One tracked workflow to a verified certificate.** APP-2026-EC-44822 goes through the stages to a named officer's approval and lands in the Locker as CRT-2026-EC-44822. This is the only fully in-app chain we captured, so it can be shown end to end without stretching anything.
3. **Same data at constituency level.** The Mylapore MLA dashboard counter goes up because this application exists. This is the reason for a Minister: governance visibility built from real records.

Together they cover the citizen, the officer and the state. These are three different viewers, one record and one geography.

5. CENTRAL VISUAL METAPHOR

**Tamil Nadu as a field of 18,165 points, where every application has an address.** The UI is not laid over the map. It is anchored at Mylapore's map coordinate (AC 25, centroid 721.3, 97.7), and the camera reaches it by zooming. This matches the product: the citizen profile carries Zone, District, SRO, Taluk and Survey 112/4B. The officer is "Mylapore Taluk", and the dashboard is "Mylapore Constituency". Every record is geographic. When the app's light ground grows out of the Mylapore polygon and later shrinks back into it, that says what the product does: a place becomes a service, and the service becomes data for that place. The points are never captioned with a count.

8. TRANSITION LOGIC

1. **Black → state:** the single gold point at frame 1 is Mylapore. The ripple starts there, so the film begins and ends on the same coordinate.
2. **Map → exploded plates:** the map itself separates. Same object, now showing its administrative layers.
3. **Plates → dive:** the plates collapse onto the point we started on. The eye already knows the target.
4. **Dark map → app ground:** the Mylapore polygon fills with #F7F7FA and grows past the frame. The constituency becomes the app canvas.
5. **Point → "I bought land":** the gold point becomes the citizen's question. A place becomes a voice.
6. **Question → scattered chips:** the chips come out of the pill's tail because they answer it.
7. **Scatter → order:** the same five chips are sorted, with the gold rule as the line of dependency. Intelligence shown as motion, not as a claim.
8. **Chip #1 → application:** we push through its "→", and the chip border becomes the card border.
9. **Submit dot → acknowledgement:** the last stepper dot becomes the green check tile.
10. **Acknowledgement → workflow:** the reference number is the token the eye follows down the spine.
11. **Spine → officer:** the officer card docks on the Approval node. Accountability has a place in the sequence.
12. **APP → CRT:** a character-level match cut where the application becomes the certificate, with the same digits 2026-EC-44822.
13. **Certificate → Locker:** the card takes the empty 5th slot.
14. **Locker → map:** the card shrinks back into the gold point and the ground shrinks back into the polygon. Exact reverse of transition 4.
15. **Map → dashboard:** the MLA plate is hinged on the Mylapore polygon. The data stands up out of the place it describes.
16. **Dashboard → state → lockup:** the plate folds flat and the ring spreads from Mylapore. The lockup takes the empty right half of the frame.

13. RISKS, FALLBACKS, ANTI-TEMPLATE

- **Geo data (blocking prep):** the `d` path strings in geo.json are **empty** for the state, districts, assemblies and taluks. Only the centroids and the 18,165 village points are filled in. Outlines have to be regenerated from assets/geo_raw/*.geojson with the stored projection (`bounds`, `k`, `scale`) and simplified (mapshaper, about 1% for constituencies, because assembly.geojson is 17 MB). This takes about an hour. Fallback: districts only, plus the Mylapore polygon.
- **Deep zoom 1×→400× with DOM UI anchored in the world (risky shot, 4.0–6.0 and 16.0–17.0):** CSS scale at that range blurs and loses precision. Render the map entirely in canvas from a JS camera matrix, using log-lerped zoom. Draw the pill and ground in canvas until the zoom passes a threshold, then swap invisibly to a DOM layer at native 1:1 with identical geometry. Fallback: hide the swap under the polygon-fill moment.
- **Exploded plates (3.0–4.0):** four canvases with translateZ. Fallback: 2D parallax offsets plus scale.
- **18k points with 180° shutter blur:** pre-render the dot sprites and use 6–8 sub-frame accumulation only during fast moves.
- **Tamil shaping:** wait for `document.fonts.ready` before the first `seek()`, and verify the conjuncts in a still frame.
- **Ward heat map:** capture 27_mla_wards actually shows the Services page, so the ward view is deliberately not used.
- **Counter values:** confirm the 4,233/4,234 pair against a fresh run before final render. If seeds change, re-capture.

How it avoids looking like a template:
- No screenshot pans: all UI is rebuilt in vector.
- No fades: everything enters by mask, morph or physical motion.
- No glow on UI chrome.
- No device frames.
- No centred title on a gradient: the lockup is asymmetric over a real map.
- No generic particles: every point is a real census location, and the ripple has a geographic origin.
- Sound drives the meaning: scattered pitches resolve into an ascending order.
- Party nods are limited to the warm ivory points, one #FFCC00 "+1" spark and the radial ripple, which is functionally the wavefront. None of them appears near the emblem.
