  Villivakkam and T. Nagar dashboards show ILLUSTRATIVE figures in the same style; each card carries a deliberately tiny,
  low-contrast 'Illustrative figures' note in its corner (the client asked that it not disturb the cinematic feel).
- The portrait is the client-supplied reference image itself (not generated); no political titles are shown.
- Scheme names/benefits are the app's product data; the complaint flow, reference format and routing rules are the app's.
- Review drafts are rendered without motion blur; the delivery master is 4K with an 8-sample 180° shutter.
