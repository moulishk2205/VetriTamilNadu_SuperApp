# THE GOLD RULE — Director's treatment and EDL (20.0 s, 1920×1080 logical, render 4K / 30 fps)

## 1. Title + logline
**The Gold Rule.** Vetri draws a 3px gold line under every one of its screens. In this film that line follows one citizen's sentence through the services, the order they come in, an officer's approval and the citizen's Locker, and ends inside the numbers of a constituency dashboard.

## 2. Product definition
Vetri Tamil Nadu is a working prototype: a citizen with one identity applies for any of 21 state services. Every application runs through one shared 15-stage workflow, and officers, departments and MLAs each see the same records in their own portal.

## 3. Primary problem
One event in a citizen's life can mean several services from several departments, and nothing tells the citizen which ones or in what order. Once an application is filed, the citizen, the officer and the elected representative usually each see a different, partial picture.

## 4. Three capabilities shown, and why these three
1. **Ask Vetri plans the order.** "I bought land" becomes 5 services across 4+ departments, sorted by what depends on what. This is the INTELLIGENCE, and it is honestly deterministic.
2. **One workflow from application to certificate.** Stepper → reference APP-2026-EC-44822 + QR → stage spine → named officer approves → certificate lands in the Digital Locker. This is the ACTION. EC is one of the 12 services that complete fully in the app, so it can finish on screen truthfully.
3. **Same data in every role.** That one application ticks the Mylapore MLA dashboard from 4,233 to 4,234. This is the IMPACT, and it is the governance reason a Minister cares.

All three are one chain from one live session. None of it is staged across separate demos.

## 5. Central visual metaphor
**The gold rule.** In the app it is the 3px line under the crimson brand bar, and the 44px rule under the selector title. In the film the same line becomes, in turn:
- the hinge the masthead opens from
- the spine of the dependency plan
- the stepper connector
- the vertical tracker spine
- the path the certificate takes to the Locker
- the district hairlines
- the closing 44px rule

It is true to the product because Vetri really is one line: one config-driven workflow (gov.standard.v1) that every application follows, from citizen to system to officer to signatory to Locker. The line isn't decoration. It is a picture of that workflow.

## 8. Transition logic (what carries the eye at each change)
1. **Black → masthead.** The gold rule is the hinge every band opens from. It is the app's own signature line.
2. **Film-scale masthead → Ask Vetri page.** One continuous pull-back. The bands *are* the real masthead, so the scale change reveals where we are.
3. **White band → input field → bubble.** The same white bar becomes the input, and the text sends into the bubble exactly as the UI does.
4. **Bubble → 21 cards.** The cards pour out of the "Services" nav item, the platform's catalogue showing up as the problem.
5. **Pile → ordered sequence.** The five real cards fly onto the returning gold rule. Disorder becoming order is the capability.
6. **Sequence → real reply.** Each card morphs into its real UI chip, so the abstraction pays off in shipped interface.
7. **Chip → stepper.** We push through the chip's own "→". Its crimson fill becomes the active-step crimson.
8. **Submit → reference.** The final step circle pops into the "submitted" check.
9. **Reference → tracker.** The gold rule leaves the card, turns a corner and becomes the stage spine. It is the same line, with application and workflow joined.
10. **Tracker → officer.** The officer card docks level with the line at Approval, so the person is placed at their stage.
11. **Confirm → completion.** A yellow spark travels the line, showing the decision moving downstream.
12. **Stage → Locker.** The "Delivered to Wallet" dot *is* the certificate. It blooms and flies into its slot.
13. **✓ → map.** The verified tick becomes a point of light, and the pull-back shows it is in Mylapore.
14. **Polygon → MLA dashboard.** The constituency shape unfolds into the constituency header, and the counters move by one: the citizen's application, in a governance view.
15. **Dashboard → state.** It folds back into its polygon and the camera rises. One constituency among 234.
16. **State → lockup.** The district hairlines converge into one gold rule, which contracts into the selector's 44 px rule. The film ends on the app's own opening geometry.

## 13. Risks, fallbacks, and avoiding a template look
| Risky shot | Risk | Fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Tamil hero animation | Splitting by glyph breaks shaping (vowel signs, pulli) | Animate whole words behind masks only. If that fails, a single-line clip-path wipe. Check against Chromium shaping before the render. |
| Push through the "→" at 14× | Glyph raster or blur at that scale | Use an SVG path, not a font glyph; render at 4K. Fallback: a crimson iris wipe from the arrow's centre. |
| 90° camera roll on the tracker | Heavy motion-blur accumulation | No roll: the line exits the bottom of the frame and enters at the top, a cut on motion with the line continuous. |
| Map zoom from AC 25 to the whole state | About 1:60 scale range, stroke widths, 18k points | Precompute projected points. Draw 1 px fillRects. Divide line widths by the zoom. Fallback: a scale-matched cut at the Chennai district outline. |
| Polygon → header morph | Irregular shape to a rounded rect | The polygon fills, then its bounding box unfolds as a maroon clip-path inset into the header. |
| Masthead opening reads as "flat slides" | — | Exact app proportions (40:74:3:47), the gradient on the crimson, the glint on the rule, hits locked to the grid. |

**What would make it look like a template:**
- centred titles on a gradient
- everything fading in
- glow on UI chrome
- particle bursts
- device mockups
- HUD corners
- "AI" sparkles

**How this film avoids each:**
- All text is left-aligned on the app's 136 px column.
- Elements arrive by wipe, pop, morph or line-draw. Opacity is used only to push things back.
- The only glow is the app's own one-breath emblem glow at the close.
- The only "particles" are real village centroids, and they aren't captioned.
- There are no frames around the UI. It is deconstructed in space.
- Every ease comes from the app (cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1), plus expo), so the motion feels born from the product, not from a preset.

**Build effort:** about 1 expert-day. The DOM UI layers are rebuilt from the captures: Ask Vetri, the stepper, the acknowledgement, the tracker, the officer action, the Locker and the MLA header + KPIs. Canvas handles the map and QR, plus one camera transform tree. A `seek(t)` timeline sits on the 96 BPM grid.
