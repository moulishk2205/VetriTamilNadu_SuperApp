# THE GOLD THREAD

## 1. Title + logline
**The Gold Thread.** One 3px gold line, the app's own masthead rule, draws Tamil Nadu, untangles a citizen's land purchase into an ordered path, carries reference APP-2026-EC-44822 through every government stage into his Digital Locker, and then reaches out to the constituency and the state.

## 2. Product definition
Vetri Tamil Nadu is a working prototype that gives each citizen one identity. With it they can apply for 21 state services, track every application through one shared government workflow, and get the result in their Digital Locker. Officers, departments, MLAs and administrators see the same records in their own portals.

## 3. Primary problem
One life event, such as buying land, needs several services from several departments, and they have to be done in a fixed order. Today the citizen has to work out which office comes first and then chase each one separately, with no view of where the application stands. (This is a structural claim only. No statistics are used, because the repo contains none.)

## 4. The three capabilities, and why these three
1. **Ask Vetri plans the order.** "I bought land" becomes 5 services in dependency order (INTELLIGENCE).
2. **One workflow from application to certificate.** A prefilled stepper, then reference APP-2026-EC-44822, then a 15-stage workflow, the officer's Approve & Issue, and a certificate in the Locker (ACTION).
3. **One record seen at every level.** The same case shows up in the Mylapore MLA dashboard, the five role portals and a platform built for 38 districts (IMPACT).

Why these three: together they are the **one real end-to-end chain captured in a single live session**. The Encumbrance Certificate (EC) is one of the 12 services that complete fully in the app, so nothing shown is staged. Each capability also maps onto one act of the brief's story spine.

## 5. Central visual metaphor
**The thread is the record.** The 3px gold rule under the crimson brand bar appears on every screen of the app. In the product, a single continuous record (one citizen ID, one reference number) passes from citizen to system to officer to signatory to Locker to dashboard. The film turns that path into a literal line and keeps the camera on it. Gold is used here as the brief allows: for light, rules and small type, never as a large fill. It is the app's own signature, so it is product-true and not decoration.

## 6. Narrative arc: 4 acts on 8 bars at 96 BPM (1 bar = 2.5 s)
- **Act I, COMPLEXITY (0.0–5.0, bars 1–2):** the thread draws Tamil Nadu, dives into Mylapore, and tangles between five scattered services. *Why it exists, and the problem.*
- **Act II, INTELLIGENCE (5.0–10.0, bars 3–4):** the knot snaps straight into the masthead rule. Ask Vetri lays the five services along it in order, and the thread becomes the stepper line. *How it works.*
- **Act III, ACTION (10.0–15.0, bars 5–6):** submission, the reference riding down the workflow, the officer's approval, the certificate going into the Locker. *What it can do.*
- **Act IV, IMPACT (15.0–20.0, bars 7–8):** the thread leaves the citizen's screen, ticks the constituency counter, rays out to the five portals and 38 districts, then gathers into the lockup rule. *Why it matters for Tamil Nadu.*

## 8. Transition logic
1. **Outline closes, then the dive (2.5):** the closing point of the loop is Chennai/Mylapore, so the camera simply follows the tip. *The state narrows down to one citizen.*
2. **Map, then service tiles (3.1):** the tiles pop around the Mylapore point the thread just reached. *The citizen's problem lives in his own locality.*
3. **Knot, then masthead rule (5.0):** the same polyline is interpolated from tangle to straight, and the app UI builds outward from it. *The product is the thing that straightens the path.* This is the film's central morph.
4. **Masthead, then Ask Vetri reply (5.6):** a branch drops from the rule and the camera tilts with it. The thrown-off tiles return in order. *Data converges into UI.*
5. **Action card, then stepper (8.75):** the camera pushes into the card's arrow, and the shaft becomes the progress line. *Choosing a service is starting it.*
6. **Stepper, then submission (10.0):** the final Submit node grows into the check tile, and the thread becomes the reference underline. *Filling in becomes a receipt.*
7. **Underline, then tracker (11.25):** the 90° roll turns horizontal progress into vertical stages, and the reference rides as a tag. *The same ID carries the whole way.*
8. **Tracker, then officer (12.5):** the officer card hangs off the thread at the Approval stage. *A named human sits on the citizen's line.*
9. **Signature ring, then certificate, then Locker (13.4–14.4):** the ring unrolls into the card's top rule, and the card docks with the four documents seen at 7.5. *A setup and its payoff.*
10. **Locker, then MLA dashboard (15.0):** a continuous lateral track along the thread with no cut. *The same record on the governance side.*
11. **Counter tick, then rays (16.6):** the rays grow out of the tile the thread just underlined. *Five roles share one record.*
12. **Dashboard, then map (17.5):** the UI scales down onto the Mylapore point, the reverse of the dive. *The film closes its own loop.*
13. **Rays, then lockup rule (18.75):** every line gathers back into the single 44px rule. *Everything is one thread again.*

## 12. Honesty audit
- **"I bought land."**, the five service names, their order, and the Tamil reply headline: chain step 1–2.
- **"Sequenced by dependency"**: the app's own line "I sequenced these by dependency." The engine is deterministic, and the film never says "AI" or "understands."
- **Departments (IGR, Revenue, local body, water board):** step 2, "span 4+ departments." Only EC→IGR is paired visually, confirmed by the submission screen. The other department labels float unpaired on purpose.
- **"4 verified documents" and their four IDs:** step 2 and step 7.
- **Stepper labels and prefilled Mylapore / Chennai / SRO Mylapore / 112/4B:** step 3. Before building, check the label set against captures 02–09; the brief says "6-step" but lists 7 labels.
- **APP-2026-EC-44822, QR, "received by Registration Department (IGR)":** step 3.
- **Stage names:** step 4. "Digital Signature" appears only as a stage name. eSign is a placeholder, so the ring is a stage marker and not a handwritten signature.
- **Officer name and title, "Approve & Issue":** step 5. The word "eSign" is never shown.

## 13. Risks, fallbacks, and how to avoid looking like a template
- **geo.json has empty paths.** `state`, district `d` and assembly `d` are all empty strings; only centroids and village points are populated. The outline must be regenerated from `assets/geo_raw/state.geojson` and `districts.geojson` using geo.json's `bounds`, `k` and `scale`, then simplified with Douglas-Peucker to about 2,000 points. This takes about 1 hour. **Fallback:** take the outline from the hull of the 18,165 village points.
- **Tamil written by the thread tip (5.6–6.9).** Real stroke-writing needs glyph outlines. **Fallback (recommended):** a mask reveal led by the tip, where the text is uncovered just behind the moving point. It reads the same at hall distance.
- **Knot-to-line morph.** Resample both shapes to N=400 points and interpolate. Draw the knot by hand as one bezier. Low risk.
- **Text sharpness during the 90° roll.** Lay out UI at its final on-screen scale and avoid `will-change`, so Chromium re-rasterizes text each frame. **Fallback:** replace the roll with a cut on the stage dot (a match cut on the green dot).
- **Shot density in bar 6 (12.5–15.0)**, the riskiest stretch. If it reads crowded, cut the notification and keep the four docked chips as plain dots.
- **Party nods.** The yellow spark appears only at the thread tip, twice (0.0 and 13.1), each for 4 frames. Radial rays appear only at 16.6–18.75 and are fully retracted before the emblem appears, so they are never paired with it. No stripes anywhere.
- **Avoiding the template look:**
  - Nothing fades in: every element is drawn by the thread, wiped out of it, or rises off it.
  - No particle bursts: the village points are real census data, lit only as the tip passes.
  - No glow on UI chrome; only the tip is hot.
  - No device frames and no centered title on a gradient.
  - The sound mirrors the story: the same five plucks go from scattered to ordered.
  - It plays as one unbroken camera move, with the cut-free lateral track at 15.0 as proof.
  - The test: if any frame could belong to another product, it fails. Every frame carries a real ID, name, place or stage from this one captured chain.
